Safety when playing in virtual reality

Play in a clear space, with no furniture, people or pets within arm’s reach, and set up your headset’s safety boundary. The games are played standing and without moving around, but they require moving your arms quickly. Take breaks and stop playing if you feel dizzy or unwell. You play at your own risk.

Privacy policy

Who is responsible

The data controllers are, jointly, the owners indicated in the legal notice. For any question about your data, write to the contact address.

The essentials

  • You don’t need to sign up to play or give any data.
  • What you do in the games (position of the headset and controllers, high scores, favorites) stays in your browser. Headset and controller movements are not sent anywhere.
  • What you write on the forum is public.
  • We don’t store your IP address: only an encrypted fingerprint used to curb spam.
  • There is no advertising and no data is sold.

User accounts

Creating an account is optional. If you create one, the following is also processed:

  • Your email, so you can sign in and so we can notify you about things related to your account (for example, that your idea has become a game). It is never shown. Email notifications can be turned off.
  • Your year of birth, to check the minimum age and protect minors. It is never shown.
  • Your nickname and avatar, which are public.
  • Your favorites, likes, high scores, suggestions, comments and the profiles you follow. You choose what appears on your public profile in the privacy settings.
  • The open sessions on your devices.

Legal basis: the account terms you accept when creating it. The data is kept as long as you have the account. If you delete it, your email, sessions, favorites, high scores and settings are erased. Your suggestions and comments remain on the forum, signed as “Deleted user”, because they are part of other people’s conversations. From “My account” you can download all your data.

If you sign in with Google, Google provides us with your email and an identifier of your Google account. We do not receive your password.

Minors

Anyone can play without giving any data. To create an account or post on the forum you must be 14 or older. Accounts of users under 18 have their follower lists hidden by default and do not receive notifications from unknown followers.

Who it is shared with

No data is sold or transferred to anyone. It is only processed by the providers that make the website work, and only for that:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website, the forum database and the visit statistics, and provides the anti-spam captcha (Turnstile).
  • jsDelivr: serves the three.js library the games run on. When loading it, it receives your IP like any web server.
  • Google Fonts: serves the fonts of the home page. When loading them, it receives your IP.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with your Google account.
  • Email delivery service: , to send you sign-in links and account notifications.

Some of these providers have servers outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, take it to another service, restrict its use or object to it. Write to the contact address saying what you want. If you posted on the forum without an account, say which suggestion or comment it was, because without an account there is no other way to know it is yours.

If you believe your data has not been handled properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency.
